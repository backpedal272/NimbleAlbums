# Nimble Albums

## Your photos. Your files. Full quality.

Nimble Albums gets your albums out of Photos and iCloud and into plain old folders on your Mac, drive, or NAS. Pick the albums, pick a folder, hit Export, and go get a coffee.

**Free on the Mac App Store** · macOS 15 Sequoia or later

---

## Why you'll like it

**📸 The real originals**
Not shrunken previews. Nimble Albums exports the full-quality original, exactly as it came off your camera. Edited a photo? You get the edited version too, saved right next to it.

**☁️ Pulls from iCloud for you**
Using "Optimize Mac Storage"? No problem. Anything that lives only in iCloud gets downloaded at full resolution automatically.

**🗂️ Folders that make sense**
One folder per album, with your Photos folders mirrored on disk. Files are named by date, like `2024-06-15_IMG_1234.heic`, so they sort themselves. Finder dates match when the shot was taken.

**✅ Tick, choose, export**
All your albums, smart albums, and shared albums in one list. Tick a whole folder in one click. Before you start, you'll see how many items there are, roughly how much space they need, and how much you have.

**🐢 Built for big exports**
Thousands of photos? Pause, resume, or cancel whenever you like. Hiccups get retried automatically, your Mac stays awake until the job is done, and you'll never end up with half-written files.

**🔁 Run it again, safely**
Exporting to the same folder twice? Choose to skip, replace, or keep both copies, once for everything.

**🔒 Private by design**
No account, no tracking, no cloud of our own. Nimble Albums only reads your library and never changes it.
